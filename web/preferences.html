Display

What does concept2upload store?
  • Your Concept2 API token - stored on our server, tied to your Google account. Nothing else about you lives there.
  • Display preferences (timezone, date/time format, how many workouts to show) - stored only in this browser, never sent to us.
  • Your workouts themselves - not stored by us at all. Every visit fetches them fresh from Concept2.
  • Your name, email and photo - managed by Google/Firebase sign-in, not stored in our database.

Danger zone

Permanently deletes your saved Concept2 token, your display preferences, and your account itself. You'll be signed out, and signing in again later creates a brand-new account - this can't be undone.
